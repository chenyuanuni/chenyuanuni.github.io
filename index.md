<section class="home-section biography" aria-labelledby="biography-title">
  <header class="section-heading"><p class="section-kicker">About</p><h2 id="biography-title">Research and background</h2></header>
  <div class="biography-copy">
    <p>I received my bachelor’s degree from the University of Electronic Science and Technology of China in 2016 and my PhD from the Singapore University of Technology and Design in 2021, supervised by Prof. Tony Quek. I previously worked with Prof. David Gesbert at EURECOM and currently work with Prof. Geyong Min at Exeter.</p>
    <p>I lead the MSCA project EIMS and have been selected for the NSFC Excellent Young Scientists Fund (Overseas). My publications include ESI top 1% highly cited papers, and I have received five best paper awards. I was named a 6G Rising Young Scientist in 2025 and an N2Women Rising Star in 2026.</p>
    <p>I serve as an Associate Editor for the IEEE Internet of Things Journal and IEEE Open Journal of the Communications Society. Alongside academic research, I work on invention patents and founded an AI-RAN startup to bring AI-native network systems into practice.</p>
  </div>
</section>
